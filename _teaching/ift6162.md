Teaching assistant for [**IFT 6162 — Building Up RL: From Dynamics and Control to Learning**](https://pierrelucbacon.com/rlbook/syllabus/), a graduate course taught by Professor Pierre-Luc Bacon that constructs reinforcement learning from first principles in dynamics, optimization, and control theory. The course covers controlled systems and state-space modeling, discrete-time optimal control, trajectory optimization and model predictive control, dynamic programming, approximate value methods, policy optimization and gradient estimation, and deep RL algorithms (DQN, DDPG, TD3, policy gradients).

- Prepared and ran weekly tutorial sessions, programming exercises, and problem sets
- Held office hours and answered student questions on the course forum
- Graded assignments and provided feedback on student projects
